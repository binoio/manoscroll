ManoScroll

Scroll a window with hand gesture recognition.

Download for macOS Requires macOS 14.0 or later •
🖐️

Gesture Control

Use open palm to scroll up and a closed fist to scroll down effortlessly.

🕵️

Background Use

Works in the background, allowing you to scroll in any active application.

⚙️

Fully Tunable

Fine-tune sensitivity, smoothing, dead zones, and detection thresholds.

Quick Start

  1. Launch ManoScroll — it appears in your menu bar and dock.
  2. Click "Start Tracking" from the menu or dock.
  3. Position your hand clearly in front of your webcam.
  4. Open palm (4-5 fingers) to scroll up; Closed fist (0-1 fingers) to scroll down.
  5. Click "Stop Tracking" when you're finished.

Permissions Required

To function correctly, ManoScroll needs two system permissions:

  • Camera Access: Required for AI-powered hand tracking and gesture detection.
  • Accessibility: Required to inject system-level scroll events into other applications.
